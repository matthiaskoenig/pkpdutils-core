A minor release: the kernel now carries the urine and the sparse analysis, so a host can run the two designs which are not a plasma timecourse without its own numerics. Its python surface is `analyze_batch`, `summarize`, `time_to_steady_state`, `nca_sparse`, `nca_urine`, `is_supported` and the three quantiles.

## New python entry points

- `nca_sparse(request)`: the destructive and the batch design, `nca_sparse` of the host. The area of the mean curve with the linear trapezoid weights, the variance and the Satterthwaite degrees of freedom from the per-animal identity of Bailer 1988 and Nedelman & Jia 1995, with the covariance of Holder 2001 for several samples per animal. It returns the parameters of the mean curve with `auc_last_se` and `auc_last_df`, and the number of batches and of singleton time points, which the host needs for its warning.
- `nca_urine(request)`: the excretion study of one subject, `nca_urine` of the host. The parameters of the excretion rate curve, the rate at the midpoint of its collection in the convention of the Phoenix urine models 210 to 212, renamed as the rate parameters (`max_rate`, `aurc_last`, `mid_pt_last` and fifteen more), with `amount_recovered`, `percent_recovered`, `vol_ur` and, when a plasma area is given, the renal clearance over the collection span.

A malformed request to either is a plain `ValueError` and never an `UnsupportedError`, as for `summarize` and `time_to_steady_state`, so a host does not mistake a wrong request for a refusal and answer it with its own path. Every entry point of the extension now routes the refusal of a unit the registry cannot read to `UnsupportedError` and every other kernel error to `ValueError` through one helper, which closes a case where such a refusal arrived as a `ValueError`: a host which falls back on `UnsupportedError` raised instead of answering, for a unit as ordinary as `grain`.

## A public API addition of the core and the abi crate

`pkpdutils-core` exports `nca_sparse` and `nca_urine`, and `pkpdutils-abi` the request types `SparseRequest`, `UrineRequest` and `CheckedUrine` with their answers. A Rust consumer may rely on them.

`CheckedUrine` is how the urine numerics takes its request: a newtype with a private field, constructible only through `UrineRequest::checked()`, which validates the collections and then sorts them. The invariant that the collections are sorted and non-overlapping is therefore carried by the type rather than by a convention, and the numerics cannot be reached with an unchecked request.

## What a host has to get right

The urine request spells the dose, the plasma area and the volumes as options, and three of them pair: `dose_amount` with `dose_unit` and `area` with `area_unit`. A non-finite `area` is a legal value and not a missing one, because the plasma area of a curve of fewer than two points is `NaN`, and the renal clearance of such a study is `NaN` rather than an error. `volume` is `null` for a study which gives the amounts directly and an array for one which gives concentrations, and the two are not the same: an all-`NaN` array is a recovered volume of `NaN`, where a missing one is no volume at all. The key is required, so a caller which speaks the wire directly has to send `"volume": null`.

The parameters of the urine analysis are the ones of a curve, so an out-of-range manual terminal point is a `ValueError`, naming the point and the number of columns. The python library raises `IndexError` there. The two will be made to agree, in the kernel's direction, as part of the error classification of a later release.

## A divergence from python, where python is the one at fault

`clr` for a plasma area of exactly 0: the kernel answers `inf` for a recovered amount over an area of 0 and `NaN` for 0 over 0. The python library raised `ZeroDivisionError`, although the block around the division suppresses the division warning and so documents that the infinity was intended. It was fixed in `pkpdutils` together with this release and is listed there as a fix.

## Compatibility

No change to `batch/1`, `analysis/1` or the options, and `analyze_batch`, `summarize`, `time_to_steady_state` and `is_supported` behave as in 0.4.1. `pkpdutils` can move its floor to this release to route the urine and the sparse analysis to the kernel.
